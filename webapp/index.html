FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate is the AI on Indian food?

Photos are matched against a database of 1,000+ verified Indian dishes with nutrition values from IFCT 2017 (India's official food composition tables) — not raw AI guesses. And nothing is saved without your confirmation, so a wrong guess never pollutes your data. Your corrections also teach the system your specific dishes and portions.

Do I need to install an app?

No. HealthSnap works entirely inside Telegram. The web dashboard is optional and can be added to your home screen like an app (PWA) — no Play Store download needed. A native Android app is on the roadmap.

Can I track my parents' health?

Yes — the Family plan covers up to 4 members. Your parents log BP/sugar by sending a simple message (or you log for them), and you see their trends and get alerts on your phone, from any city.

Is my health data private?

Your logs are stored in your own private account and never sold or shared. You can export or delete all your data anytime. Payments are handled by Razorpay — we never see your card or UPI details.

Is this medical advice?

No. HealthSnap is a tracking and awareness tool. BP/sugar classifications follow standard guideline ranges and the AI summary is general wellness guidance — always consult your doctor for medical decisions.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

You drop to the Free plan automatically — no auto-charge without consent. Upgrade to Pro anytime from the bot with UPI in under a minute.
