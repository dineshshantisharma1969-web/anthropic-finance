4. Authorisation of officers of State tax or Union territory tax as proper officer in 
certain circumstances. 
Without prejudice to the provisions of this Act, the officers appointed under the State Goods 
and Services Tax Act or the Union Territory Goods and Services Tax Act are authorised to be 
the proper officers for the purposes of this Act, subject to such exceptions and conditions as the 
Government shall, on the recommendations of the Council, by notification, specify.
