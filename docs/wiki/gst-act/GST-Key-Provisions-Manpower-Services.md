# Key GST Provisions for ISPL — Manpower & Facility Services

The GST law as it actually applies to a manpower / security / housekeeping / facility
services company. Companion to the [full section index](CGST-Act-Section-Index.md).

> ⚠️ Positions below are as amended up to the Finance Act, 2025. Verify wording and
> current notification status on [CBIC](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in) before
> relying on it in a notice reply or filing.

---

## 1. What we charge and on what value

| Point | Position | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Classification | Manpower/labour supply **SAC 9985** (998519); security services **998529**; cleaning/housekeeping under 9985/99853x | Scheme of classification, Notification 11/2017-CT(Rate) |
| Rate | **18%** (9% CGST + 9% SGST, or 18% IGST inter-State) | Notification 11/2017-CT(Rate) |
| Charge mechanism | **Forward charge** — ISPL is a body corporate, so even security services are billed with GST by us (RCM applies only when a *non-body-corporate* supplies security personnel to a registered person) | s.9(3) + Notification 13/2017 as amended by 29/2018-CT(Rate) |
| **Taxable value** | GST applies on the **gross bill — wages + PF + ESI + service charge**, not on service charge alone. "Reimbursement of salary" is still consideration unless strict **pure agent** conditions are met (they almost never are for manpower supply) | s.15; Rule 33 CGST Rules; consistent AAR rulings |
| Invoice timing | Tax invoice within **30 days** of supply of service | s.31, Rule 47 |
| E-invoicing | **Mandatory** (turnover > ₹5 crore since 01-08-2023 — ISPL is well above) | Rule 48(4) |

## 2. Exemptions we may actually touch

| Situation | Treatment | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Security/cleaning/housekeeping **to a school up to higher secondary** | **Exempt** | Notification 12/2017-CT(Rate), entry 66 |
| **Pure services to Government / local authority** relating to Panchayat (Art. 243G) or Municipality (Art. 243W) functions | **Exempt** — check each govt contract's scope | Notification 12/2017, entry 3 |
| Services **by employees to ISPL** (our own payroll) | Not a supply at all — salary is outside GST | **Schedule III** |
| Clients in exempt sectors (hospitals/healthcare) | Our supply is still **taxable at 18%**; the client just cannot claim ITC — affects pricing negotiations, not our liability | s.9 |

## 3. Our compliance calendar

| Return | What | Due |
|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 | Outward supplies (invoice-level) | 11th of next month |
| GSTR-3B | Tax payment return | 20th of next month |
| GSTR-9 / 9C | Annual return + reconciliation statement | 31 December after FY end |
| GSTR-2B | Auto-drafted ITC statement — **ITC only if invoice appears here** (s.16(2)(aa)) | Generated ~14th |

**ITC discipline (s.16):** invoice in 2B + goods/services received + supplier has paid
tax + we pay the supplier within **180 days** (else reverse with interest) + claim by
**30 November** of the following FY (s.16(4)) — after that the credit is dead.
Blocked credits list: **s.17(5)**.

## 4. Government contracts — GST TDS (s.51)

Government departments, local authorities and PSUs deduct **2% GST TDS** (1% CGST +
1% SGST) on payments where the contract value exceeds ₹2.5 lakh. They file
**GSTR-7**; the credit lands in our **electronic cash ledger** — accept it monthly
and reconcile against receivables. Unclaimed TDS sitting in the cash ledger is
refundable (s.54).

## 5. Interest, late fee, demands — the notice pipeline

This maps directly to the **IT & GST case tracker** workflow.

| Form | What it means | Governing section | Typical response window |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSTR-3A | Return not filed | s.46 | 15 days (file the return) |
| ASMT-10 | Scrutiny discrepancy in returns | s.61 | 30 days (reply in ASMT-11) |
| ADT-01 | Departmental audit intimation | s.65 | As stated |
| DRC-01A | Pre-SCN intimation (pay or contest) | Rule 142(1A) | As stated — cheapest exit point |
| DRC-01 | Show-cause notice (demand) | s.73 / s.74 / s.74A | 30 days usually |
| DRC-07 | Demand order summary | s.73/74/74A | Appeal window starts |
| DRC-03 | Our voluntary payment form | — | — |

**Demand time limits:**

- **s.73 (non-fraud, up to FY 2023-24):** order within 3 years of the annual-return
  due date; penalty 10% of tax (min ₹10,000) — **zero penalty** if tax + interest
  paid within 30 days of the SCN.
- **s.74 (fraud/suppression, up to FY 2023-24):** 5 years; penalty up to 100%
  (reduces to 15% before SCN / 25% within 30 days of SCN / 50% within 30 days of order).
- **s.74A (FY 2024-25 onwards):** single unified provision — SCN within 42 months of
  the annual-return due date, order within 12 months of the SCN (extendable 6).
- **Interest (s.50):** 18% p.a. — on the cash-paid portion where a 3B is filed late.
- **s.128A amnesty:** interest & penalty waived for s.73 demands of FY 2017-18 to
  2019-20 where full tax was paid by the notified date — check status of any old
  cases still in the tracker.

**Service of notice (s.169):** upload on the GST portal is valid service — the
portal's *Additional Notices and Orders* tab must be checked routinely, not just
email/post. Deadlines run from portal upload.

## 7. Odds and ends that bite

- **Multi-State operations:** each State registration is a **distinct person**
  (s.25(4)); cross-branch support services can be a taxable supply under
  **Schedule I** even with no invoice raised.
- **Directors' personal liability (s.89):** directors of a private company can be
  made personally liable for unrecoverable GST dues unless non-neglect is proved.
- **Record retention (s.36):** 72 months from the annual-return due date — align the
  salary/reconciliation archive policy with this.
- **Registered office / additional places of business** must match where records
  are kept (s.35).
