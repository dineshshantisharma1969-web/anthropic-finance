# CGST Act, 2017 — Chapter & Section Index

Complete map of the **Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017** (as amended up to
the Finance Act, 2025). One line per section — enough to find the right provision
fast; read the full text on [CBIC](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in) /
[India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in) before quoting it in a filing or a
notice reply.

> ⭐ = sections most frequently relevant to ISPL day-to-day (see the
> [Key Provisions page](GST-Key-Provisions-Manpower-Services.md)).

## Schedules

| Schedule | Subject |
|---|---|
| **I** | Activities treated as supply **even without consideration** (e.g. related-party/branch supplies — relevant for cross-State branch billing) |

## Related GST statutes (same family, same structure)

| Act | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **IGST Act, 2017** | Inter-State supplies, place of supply (ss. 10–13), zero-rating/exports (s.16) |
| **UTGST Act, 2017** | Union Territory GST |
| **State GST Acts** (e.g. MGST Act, 2017 — Maharashtra) | State mirror of the CGST Act, near-identical section numbering |
| **GST (Compensation to States) Act, 2017** | Compensation cess |
| **CGST Rules, 2017** | All procedure — registration, invoice, e-invoice, e-way bill, returns, refunds, DRC forms |

*Because the State Acts mirror the CGST Act, a section reference like "s.73" reads the
same in a Maharashtra (MGST) notice as in a central one.*
