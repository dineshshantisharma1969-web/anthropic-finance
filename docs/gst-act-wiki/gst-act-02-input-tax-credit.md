# GST Act — 2. Input Tax Credit (Sections 16–21, Chapter V)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Basics, Levy & Supply](gst-act-01-basics-levy-supply.md)

## Section 16 — Eligibility and conditions

ITC is available on goods/services used in the course or furtherance of business, subject to **all** of:

1. **16(2)(a)** — possession of a valid tax invoice/debit note;
2. **16(2)(aa)** — the invoice is **furnished by the supplier in GSTR-1 and appears in the recipient's GSTR-2B** (the matching condition — vendor follow-up is a statutory necessity, not just hygiene);
3. **16(2)(b)** — goods/services actually received;
4. **16(2)(ba)** — credit is **not restricted in GSTR-2B** under s.38;
5. **16(2)(c)** — tax has actually been paid to Government by the supplier;
6. **16(2)(d)** — recipient has filed the return (GSTR-3B).

- **180-day rule (2nd proviso):** if the supplier is not paid within 180 days of invoice, ITC + interest must be reversed (re-creditable on payment).
- **16(3):** no ITC on the tax component if depreciation is claimed on it under Income-tax.
- **16(4) time limit:** ITC for a financial year must be claimed by **30 November following the FY** (or the annual return, if earlier).
- **16(5)/(6)** *(inserted retrospectively by Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024)*: extended time limit for FY 2017-18 to 2020-21 — ITC claimed in any GSTR-3B filed up to **30 Nov 2021** is valid; special window for returns filed after revocation of cancelled registration. Basis of the s.128A-linked relief for old ITC demands.

## Section 17 — Apportionment and blocked credits

- **17(1)–(2):** ITC restricted to business use and to taxable (incl. zero-rated) supplies; proportionate reversal for exempt supplies per Rules 42/43.
- **17(3):** exempt supplies include RCM-taxed inward supplies for this purpose; Schedule III items generally excluded (land, and duty-paid warehoused sale entries specifically included).
- **17(4):** banks/financial institutions — 50% option.
- **17(5) Blocked credits** — the audit-favourite list:
  - motor vehicles for passenger transport (≤13 seats) unless used for further supply/passenger transport/training (and related insurance, repair);
  - food & beverages, catering, health services, memberships of clubs/fitness, life & health insurance (allowed where obligatory under any law — relevant for ESI-uncovered employee mediclaim mandates), leave/home travel benefits;
  - **works contract services for immovable property** (other than plant & machinery), and goods/services for **construction on own account**;
  - composition-scheme inward supplies, non-resident taxable person supplies;
  - **CSR-spend goods/services — blocked w.e.f. 1 Oct 2023 (17(5)(fa))**;
  - personal consumption; goods lost/stolen/destroyed/written off or given as **gifts or free samples**;
  - tax paid under ss. 74 (fraud demands), 129, 130.

## Section 18 — Credit in special circumstances

Fresh registration, voluntary registration, composition→regular switch, exempt→taxable: ITC on stock allowed (1-year-old invoice cap). Reverse on cancellation/switch to composition. **18(6):** supply of capital goods after use — pay higher of (ITC less 5%/quarter) or tax on transaction value.

## Section 19 — Job work

ITC allowed on inputs/capital goods sent to job worker (even directly); deemed supply if not returned within **1 year (inputs) / 3 years (capital goods)** — extendable.

## Section 20 — Input Service Distributor

Distribution of common input-service credit pro-rata to turnover of recipients. **Mandatory (not optional) from 1 April 2025** for common third-party services received for multiple GSTINs, including RCM invoices — a head-office receiving common services (audit fees, software, insurance) must register as ISD and distribute; cross-charge is no longer a substitute for these.
