# GST Act — 7. Appeals, Offences & Miscellaneous (Sections 85–174, Chapters XVI–XXI)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Assessment, Audit, Demands & Recovery](gst-act-06-assessment-audit-demands.md)

## Chapter XVI — Liability in Certain Cases (ss. 85–94)

Transfer of business (transferor & transferee jointly liable, s.85); amalgamation (s.87); **liquidation — directors of private companies personally liable unless non-neglect proved (s.88(3)/89)**; partners jointly & severally liable (s.90); guardians/trustees (s.91–92); death — legal representatives liable from the estate (s.93).

## Chapter XVII — Advance Ruling (ss. 95–106)

Binding ruling on classification, rate, ITC admissibility, registration liability, "is it a supply". AAR (s.96) → appeal to AAAR within 30 days (s.100). Binding only on the **applicant and their jurisdictional officer** (s.103); void if obtained by suppression (s.104). Useful for rate/classification doubts on new service lines before contracting.

## Chapter XVIII — Appeals and Revision (ss. 107–121)

| Forum | Section | Time limit | Pre-deposit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appellate Authority (Commissioner Appeals) | 107 | 3 months (+1 condonable) | **10%** of disputed tax (max ₹20 cr) |
| **GST Appellate Tribunal (GSTAT)** | 109–113 | 3 months from order (operational timelines notified as the benches commenced from 2025) | additional **10%** (max ₹20 cr; reduced caps notified) |
| High Court (substantial question of law) | 117 | 180 days | — |
| Supreme Court | 118 | — | — |

- **s.107(11):** appellate authority cannot remand — must decide.
- **s.108:** revisional powers of Commissioner against prejudicial-to-revenue orders.
- **s.114–116:** Tribunal procedures; authorised representatives.
- **s.119/121:** sums due payable notwithstanding appeal; non-appealable orders (transfer, seizure procedure, etc.).
- Recovery is generally **stayed** on payment of pre-deposit (s.107(7)/112(9)).

## Chapter XIX — Offences and Penalties (ss. 122–138)

- **s.122** — the general penalty menu (supply without invoice, invoice without supply, ITC without receipt, TDS failures, etc.): higher of ₹10,000 or the tax evaded. **122(1A):** beneficiary of fake-invoice transactions — penalty equal to tax. **122A/122B:** special penalties for unregistered machines / track-and-trace violations (notified sectors).
- **s.123–127:** returns non-filing on notice (s.123 → up to ₹5,000/₹10,000 slabs), record failures, **general residual penalty ₹25,000 (s.125)**.
- **s.126 — Discipline:** no penalty for minor breaches (tax < ₹5,000) or bona fide documentation errors; penalty proportional to severity.
- **s.128** — power to waive penalties; **s.128A** — the 2024 amnesty (interest/penalty waiver, FY 17-18 to 19-20 s.73 demands).
- **s.129** — detention of goods in transit: penalty 200% of tax (owner comes forward); **s.130** confiscation & fine in lieu.
- **s.132 — Prosecution:** fake invoicing, tax collected-not-paid > 6 months, etc.; imprisonment up to 5 years (>₹5 cr evasion — cognizable, non-bailable). Decriminalisation (2023): prosecution threshold ₹2 crore (except fake invoices at ₹1 crore); obstruction/evidence-tampering offences dropped.
- **s.137** — offences by companies: officers in charge liable. **s.138 — Compounding** (₹...% slabs; fake-invoice issuers excluded).

## Chapter XX — Transitional Provisions (ss. 139–142)

Migration of pre-GST registrations (s.139); **TRAN-1/TRAN-2 credits** — carry-forward of CENVAT/VAT credit (s.140, incl. the *Filco Trade* reopening window of 2022); goods in transit, job work stock, pending refunds/proceedings under old law (ss.141–142). Now mostly of litigation-history value.

## Chapter XXI — Miscellaneous (ss. 143–174) — highlights

- **s.143** Job-work procedure. **s.144–145** presumptions on documents.
- **s.148** special procedures notification power (basis of many schemes).
- **s.150–151** information returns / power to call information.
- **s.155 — Burden of proof for ITC lies on the claimant.**
- **s.158A** consent-based data sharing (account aggregators/OCEN).
- **s.160–161** — proceedings not invalid for mistakes; **rectification of errors apparent on record within 3 months (max 6)** — the quick fix for arithmetic errors in orders.
- **s.168A** — COVID-era limitation extensions (source of much notice-validity litigation).
- **s.171 — Anti-profiteering** (rate-cut/ITC benefit pass-through; sunset for new complaints from 1 April 2025, cases moved to GSTAT principal bench).
- **s.172** removal-of-difficulties; **s.173–174** repeal & saving of old indirect-tax laws.
