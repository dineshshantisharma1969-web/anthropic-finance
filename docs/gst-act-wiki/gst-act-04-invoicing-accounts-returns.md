# GST Act — 4. Invoicing, Accounts & Returns (Sections 31–48, Chapters VII–IX)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Registration](gst-act-03-registration.md)

## Chapter VII — Tax Invoice, Credit and Debit Notes (ss. 31–34)

- **Section 31 — Tax invoice.** Services: invoice within **30 days** of supply. Continuous supply of services (typical monthly manpower contracts): invoice on/before the due date of payment per contract, or on receipt of payment, or on completion of the milestone. RCM inward from unregistered persons → recipient issues **self-invoice** plus payment voucher.
- **E-invoicing (Rule 48(4)):** mandatory IRN for B2B invoices where aggregate turnover exceeded **₹5 crore** in any FY from 2017-18. An invoice without IRN (when applicable) is not a valid invoice — client ITC fails.
- **Section 31A:** notified suppliers must offer prescribed electronic payment modes.
- **Section 32:** unregistered persons must not collect tax.
- **Section 33:** tax amount to be shown separately in the invoice.
- **Section 34 — Credit and debit notes.** Credit notes (deficiency, discount, sales return): declare by **30 November following the FY**; output liability reduction allowed only if the recipient reverses corresponding ITC (now tracked via the portal's IMS). Debit notes: no time cap for issuance; recipient's ITC time limit runs from the **debit note's** date (s.16(4)).

## Chapter VIII — Accounts and Records (ss. 35–36)

- **Section 35:** books at each registered place of business — production, inward/outward supplies, stock, ITC, output tax, payments. Electronic records permitted. ~~35(5) GST audit by CA/CMA~~ **omitted from FY 2020-21** — replaced by self-certified GSTR-9C.
- **Section 36 — Retention:** **72 months** from the due date of the annual return; longer if appeal/proceedings pending (1 year after disposal).

## Chapter IX — Returns (ss. 37–48)

| Section | Return | What it is | Periodicity / due date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 37 | **GSTR-1** | Outward supplies | Monthly 11th (QRMP: quarterly 13th, with IFF) |
| 38 | **GSTR-2B** | Auto-drafted ITC statement (with restricted-credit flags) | Auto, 14th |
| 39 | **GSTR-3B** | Summary return + payment | Monthly 20th (QRMP: 22nd/24th) |
| 39 | GSTR-7 | TDS return (deductors) | 10th |
| 39 | GSTR-8 | TCS return (e-commerce) | 10th |
| 44 | **GSTR-9/9C** | Annual return + reconciliation | 31 December following FY |
| 45 | GSTR-10 | Final return on cancellation | 3 months from cancellation |

- **s.37(3)/39(9) amendments:** errors correctable up to **30 November following the FY**. **s.37(4)/39(10):** sequential filing — GSTR-1 blocked if prior GSTR-1 pending; GSTR-3B blocked if GSTR-1 not filed.
- **Three-year bar:** returns under ss. 37, 39, 44, 45 **cannot be filed after 3 years** from their due date (Finance Act 2023, operationalised on the portal from mid-2025) — old unfiled returns become permanently unfilable.
- **Section 40:** first return. **s.41:** self-assessed ITC availment; reversal if supplier hasn't paid. *(ss. 42, 43, 43A — the original matching mechanism — omitted.)*
- **Section 46:** notice to non-filers (GSTR-3A) → 15 days, else best-judgment assessment u/s 62.
- **Section 47 — Late fee:** per-day late fee per Act (capped by notifications, slabbed by turnover).
- **Section 48 — GST practitioners.**

### Monthly compliance rhythm (per GSTIN)

1. By 11th — GSTR-1 (match with e-invoice/IRN data and revenue register).
2. 14th — pull GSTR-2B; run vendor-wise ITC reconciliation; act on IMS accept/reject.
3. By 20th — GSTR-3B: output (incl. RCM on security services/GTA/advocate fees), ITC per 2B, reversals (Rule 42/43, 180-day rule), cash payment.
4. TDS credits (government clients) — accept GSTR-7 credits in the cash ledger.
5. Annually — GSTR-9 & 9C by 31 December; ITC true-up by 30 November.
