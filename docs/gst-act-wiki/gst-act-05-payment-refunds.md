# GST Act — 5. Payment, TDS/TCS & Refunds (Sections 49–58, Chapters X–XI)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Invoicing, Accounts & Returns](gst-act-04-invoicing-accounts-returns.md)

## Chapter X — Payment of Tax (ss. 49–53A)

- **Section 49 — Ledgers.**
  - *Electronic cash ledger* — deposits via challan (PMT-06); usable for tax, interest, penalty, fees. Transfers between heads/GSTINs of the same PAN via **PMT-09** (s.49(10)).
  - *Electronic credit ledger* — ITC; usable **only for output tax** (never for interest, penalty, late fee, or RCM liability — **RCM must be paid in cash**).
  - Credit utilisation order (s.49(5) + s.49A/49B, Rule 88A): IGST credit first (against IGST, then CGST/SGST in any order); CGST↔SGST cross-utilisation prohibited.
  - **Rule 86B:** where monthly taxable turnover > ₹50 lakh, at least 1% of output liability in cash (exemptions incl. >₹1 lakh income-tax paid in each of last 2 years).
- **Section 50 — Interest.** 18% p.a. on delayed payment — on the **net cash** portion for delayed GSTR-3B (retrospective). ITC **wrongly availed and utilised**: 18% (s.50(3) as amended; interest only on utilisation, not mere availment). No interest where cash-ledger balance covered the liability (Rule 88B relief).
- **Section 51 — TDS.** Government departments, local authorities, PSUs and notified persons deduct **2% (1%+1%)** on payments against contracts with taxable value > **₹2.5 lakh**. No deduction where supplier's State ≠ place of supply (registration State mismatch). Deductor files **GSTR-7** by the 10th; credit flows to the supplier's **cash ledger**. *ISPL angle: most government security/manpower contracts suffer GST TDS — accept credits monthly and net them against 3B cash payable; reconcile GSTR-7 credits against receivables.*
- **Section 52 — TCS.** E-commerce operators collect 0.5% on net taxable supplies made through them.
- **Section 53/53A:** inter-government fund transfers (internal plumbing).

## Chapter XI — Refunds (ss. 54–58)

- **Section 54 — Refund.** Application (RFD-01) within **2 years** from the relevant date. Main heads:
  - zero-rated supplies (exports/SEZ) — with or without LUT (s.16 IGST Act);
  - **inverted duty structure** (input rate > output rate) — formula-based, **input services excluded** from the net-ITC formula (as upheld in *VKC Footsteps*);
  - excess cash-ledger balance (no 2-year bar);
  - excess payment, finalisation of provisional assessment, appeal-order refunds.
  - Refunds > ₹2 lakh need CA/CMA certificate (unless zero-rated categories); **unjust enrichment** test applies, else credit to Consumer Welfare Fund (s.57).
- **Section 55:** refunds to UN bodies/embassies.
- **Section 56 — Interest on delayed refund:** 6% beyond 60 days from complete application; 9% where pursuant to an appellate order.
- **Section 58:** utilisation of Consumer Welfare Fund.

### Practical notes

- A pure domestic services company rarely has refund claims other than **excess cash ledger** (common where GST TDS credits pile up beyond output liability in low-billing months) — claim these, they are near-automatic.
- Refund of cash-ledger TDS accumulation: file under "excess balance in electronic cash ledger".
