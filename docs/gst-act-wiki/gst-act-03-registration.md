# GST Act — 3. Registration (Sections 22–30, Chapter VI)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Input Tax Credit](gst-act-02-input-tax-credit.md)

## Section 22 — Persons liable for registration

Registration required in every State from which taxable supplies are made once **aggregate turnover** (all-India, PAN-level) exceeds:

| Category | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Services (or mixed goods+services) | **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh in special-category States: Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura) |
| Exclusively goods | ₹40 lakh (subject to State opt-ins and exclusions) |

A manpower company supplying from multiple States needs a **separate GSTIN in each State of supply** — turnover is aggregated pan-India for the threshold, but registration is State-wise.

## Section 23 — Not liable

Persons supplying only exempt/non-taxable supplies; agriculturists. s.23 overrides ss.22/24 (retrospective amendment from 1 July 2017).

## Section 24 — Compulsory registration (threshold irrelevant)

Inter-state taxable suppliers (services get threshold relief by notification), casual taxable persons, **persons liable under RCM**, e-commerce operators/suppliers, non-residents, **persons required to deduct TDS u/s 51**, ISD, OIDAR suppliers, agents.

## Section 25 — Procedure

State-wise registration; multiple registrations per State allowed for separate places of business. **Aadhaar authentication** mandatory for registration (physical verification if not authenticated). Distinct persons: each GSTIN of the same PAN is a distinct person — inter-branch supplies are taxable (Schedule I).

## Section 26 — Deemed registration

SGST registration deemed CGST registration and vice versa.

## Section 27 — Casual/non-resident: 90-day validity, advance tax deposit.

## Section 28 — Amendment

Apply within 15 days of change; core-field changes need officer approval.

## Section 29 — Cancellation or suspension

- By taxpayer: business discontinued/transferred, turnover below threshold.
- By officer: contravention, **non-filing of returns for 6 months** (continuous default), registration by fraud. Registration is **suspended** during proceedings — no taxable supplies/invoices during suspension.
- Cancellation does not extinguish past liabilities; ITC reversal on stock/capital goods required.

## Section 30 — Revocation of cancellation

Apply within **90 days** (extendable) of cancellation order. All pending returns must be filed first. See s.16(6) for the ITC window after revocation.

### Practical checklist (ISPL)

- New State contract → obtain registration **before** first supply from that State (or bill inter-state from an existing GSTIN where the contract permits — place-of-supply analysis under IGST Act ss.10/12 needed).
- Track return-filing continuity per GSTIN — a 6-month default triggers suo-motu cancellation and blocks e-way bills/e-invoices.
- Client-side: verify vendor GSTIN status monthly (cancelled vendor = ITC risk under s.16(2)(c)).
