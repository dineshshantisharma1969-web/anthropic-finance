# GST Act — 8. Schedules & Rate Structure

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Appeals, Offences & Miscellaneous](gst-act-07-appeals-offences-misc.md)

## Schedule I — Supplies without consideration that are still "supply"

1. Permanent transfer/disposal of business assets **where ITC was availed**.
2. Supplies between **related persons or distinct persons** (branches under different GSTINs) in the course of business — *this is why inter-State branch cross-charges are taxable*. Employer→employee gifts up to ₹50,000/year excluded.
3. Principal↔agent supplies of goods (where the agent invoices in own name).
4. Import of services from a related person/establishment abroad, for business.

## Schedule II — Classification as goods vs services

Key entries: transfer of title = goods; transfer of right to use = services; lease/tenancy/licence to occupy land = service; **works contract = service**; **restaurant/catering = service**; transfer of business assets; temporary transfer of IP = service; agreeing to refrain/tolerate an act = service (the "liquidated damages" entry — CBIC Circular 178/2022 narrows taxability of contractual penalties/notice-pay recovery).

## Schedule III — Neither goods nor services (no GST)

1. **Services by an employee to the employer** in the course of employment — the line that keeps salaries out of GST (contract labour supplied *by a contractor* is NOT employment vis-à-vis the client — fully taxable).
2. Courts/tribunals; MP/MLA/constitutional-post functions; funeral/burial; **sale of land and completed buildings**; actionable claims **other than specified actionable claims** (from Oct 2023, casino/online-money-gaming/betting are taxable); high-seas sales, bonded-warehouse transfers before clearance, merchanting trade (items 7–8, with no ITC-reversal exception for 8(a)).

## Rate structure (post the September 2025 rationalisation, effective 22-09-2025)

The 56th GST Council meeting collapsed the four main slabs into two, plus a demerit rate:

| Slab | Typical coverage |
|---|---|
| **0% / exempt** | Unbranded essentials, healthcare, education; individual health & life insurance (exempted from Sep 2025) |
| **5% (merit)** | Essentials, many mass-consumption goods, job work entries, transport options without ITC |
| **18% (standard)** | **Most services — including manpower supply, security, housekeeping (SAC 9985/9987 group)**; most goods |
| **40% (demerit)** | Pan masala, tobacco, aerated/caffeinated drinks, luxury vehicles, online money gaming etc. (compensation cess merged) |

> Always confirm the current rate from Notification 1/2017-CT(R) & 11/2017-CT(R) as amended (goods/services respectively) — the tables above are orientation, not authority.

## Manpower/security services — the rate & mechanism cheat-sheet

| Supply | SAC | Rate | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply of manpower / labour (to any client) | 998519 | 18% | **Forward charge** |
| Security services (supplier is a company/body corporate) | 998529 | 18% | Forward charge |
| Security services (supplier is proprietorship/partnership/LLP → registered recipient) | 998529 | 18% | **RCM** on recipient |
| Housekeeping/cleaning services | 998533/9985 | 18% | Forward charge (RCM only via e-commerce u/s 9(5)) |
| Payroll/HR support services | 99851 | 18% | Forward charge |

- Billing model: gross billing (wages + statutory + service charge) is fully taxable at 18% unless **pure agent** conditions (Rule 33) are strictly documented — separate authorisation, actual-cost pass-through, separate disclosure.
- Government clients: check the exemption notification's *pure services to government* entry for the specific contract before billing exempt — the entry has been narrowed and most manpower supplies to government are **taxable**, with **2% GST TDS u/s 51** on top.
