# GST Act — 1. Basics, Levy & Supply (Sections 1–15)

[← Wiki Home](README.md)

## Chapter I — Preliminary (ss. 1–2)

- **Section 1 — Short title, extent, commencement.** CGST Act, 2017; extends to the whole of India. Most provisions effective 1 July 2017.
- **Section 2 — Definitions.** 120+ definitions. The ones used daily:
  - **2(6) aggregate turnover** — all-India PAN-level turnover: taxable + exempt + exports + inter-state supplies between distinct persons; *excludes* inward RCM supplies and GST itself. Drives registration threshold and composition eligibility.
  - **2(17) business** — very wide; includes anything incidental/ancillary.
  - **2(31) consideration** — payment in money or otherwise, including subsidies (other than government subsidies); a deposit is not consideration until applied.
  - **2(47) exempt supply** — nil-rated + wholly exempt + non-taxable supply.
  - **2(52) goods** / **2(102) services** — services = anything other than goods, money, securities (but includes conversion/exchange of money for a fee).
  - **2(61) Input Service Distributor (ISD)** — office distributing credit of input services to branches. **Mandatory from 1 April 2025** for third-party invoices of common services (Finance Act 2024 amendment).
  - **2(62) input tax**, **2(63) ITC**.
  - **2(83) outward supply**, **2(93) recipient**, **2(105) supplier**.
  - **2(98) reverse charge** — recipient liable to pay tax, under s.9(3)/(4) CGST or s.5(3)/(4) IGST.
  - **2(107) taxable person**, **2(112) turnover in State**.

## Chapter II — Administration (ss. 3–6)

- **ss. 3–5:** Appointment, powers of officers (Commissioner down to inspector).
- **s. 6:** Cross-empowerment — State officers may act under the CGST Act and vice versa; no parallel proceedings on the same subject by both administrations.

## Chapter III — Levy and Collection (ss. 7–11)

- **Section 7 — Supply.** The taxable event. Includes sale, transfer, barter, exchange, licence, rental, lease, disposal for consideration in the course of business; import of services for consideration (business or not); Schedule I activities (no consideration); read with Schedule II (classification as goods vs services) and Schedule III (neither goods nor services). See [Schedules page](gst-act-08-schedules-and-rates.md).
- **Section 8 — Composite and mixed supplies.**
  - *Composite* (naturally bundled, one principal supply) → taxed at the principal supply's rate.
  - *Mixed* (bundled for a single price, not naturally) → taxed at the highest rate among components.
- **Section 9 — Levy.** CGST on intra-state supplies at notified rates (max 20% CGST); petroleum crude, diesel, petrol, natural gas, ATF kept outside until notified; alcohol for human consumption outside GST entirely.
  - **9(3) RCM (notified supplies).** Key entries for a manpower company: **security services** (supplier other than body corporate → registered recipient pays), GTA, advocate services, sponsorship, director services, renting of motor vehicle (non-corporate to corporate). *Note: supply of manpower/housekeeping per se is forward charge — only security services are in the RCM list.*
  - **9(4) RCM (unregistered suppliers).** Now confined to notified classes — practically, promoters in real estate. Not a general levy since 1 Feb 2019.
  - **9(5) E-commerce operators** liable as if supplier: passenger transport, restaurant supplies, housekeeping via platforms.
- **Section 10 — Composition levy.** Optional for turnover ≤ ₹1.5 crore (goods; 1% traders/manufacturers, 5% restaurants) and the 6% service-provider scheme under s.10(2A) up to ₹50 lakh. Not available for inter-state outward supplies — rarely relevant to ISPL.
- **Section 11 — Power to exempt** by notification (e.g., Notification 12/2017-CT(R) service exemptions). Pure services to government under specified articles were exempt; that entry has been progressively narrowed — check the current notification text before claiming exemption on government manpower contracts.

## Chapter IV — Time and Value of Supply (ss. 12–15)

- **Section 12 — Time of supply of goods.** Earlier of invoice date (or last date to issue invoice) and payment receipt; for RCM goods — earliest of receipt of goods, payment, or 31st day from supplier invoice.
- **Section 13 — Time of supply of services.** Earlier of invoice (if issued within 30 days of service) and payment; else provision of service vs payment, whichever earlier. **RCM services:** earlier of payment date or **61st day** from supplier's invoice — this drives when RCM on security-service purchases must hit the liability ledger.
- **Section 14 —** Change in rate: two-out-of-three rule (supply, invoice, payment) determines applicable rate.
- **Section 15 — Value of supply.** Transaction value where price is sole consideration and parties unrelated. Includes: taxes other than GST, amounts supplier was liable for but recipient paid, incidental expenses, interest/late fee/penalty for delayed payment, direct-linked subsidies. Excludes: discounts on invoice, and post-supply discounts only if pre-agreed and ITC reversed by recipient. Related-party/branch valuation → Valuation Rules (Rules 27–35). For manpower billing, reimbursements (wages, PF, ESI billed to client) form part of value unless the strict **pure agent** conditions (Rule 33) are met — most wage-plus-service-charge contracts are fully taxable on the gross amount.
