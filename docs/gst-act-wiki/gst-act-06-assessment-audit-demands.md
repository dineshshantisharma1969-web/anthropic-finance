# GST Act — 6. Assessment, Audit, Demands & Recovery (Sections 59–84, Chapters XII–XV)

[← Wiki Home](README.md) · [← Prev: Payment, TDS/TCS & Refunds](gst-act-05-payment-refunds.md)

## Chapter XII — Assessment (ss. 59–64)

- **s.59** Self-assessment — the default; returns are self-assessed.
- **s.60** Provisional assessment — on taxpayer request (valuation/rate doubt), bond + security; finalise in 6 months.
- **s.61** Scrutiny of returns — **ASMT-10** notice for discrepancies → reply in ASMT-11 → officer accepts (ASMT-12) or escalates to s.65/66/67 or s.73/74/74A.
- **s.62** Best-judgment assessment of **non-filers** (after s.46 notice). Order deemed withdrawn if the return is filed within **60 days** (plus 60 more with late fee).
- **s.63** Assessment of unregistered persons — 5-year window.
- **s.64** Summary assessment in special cases (revenue-risk), with s.62-style safeguards.

## Chapter XIII — Audit (ss. 65–66)

- **s.65** Departmental audit — 15 working days' notice (ADT-01); complete in 3 months (extendable to 6); findings in **ADT-02**.
- **s.66** Special audit by nominated CA/CMA where valuation/credit complexity suspected.

## Chapter XIV — Inspection, Search, Seizure, Arrest (ss. 67–72)

- **s.67** Inspection/search with Joint-Commissioner authorisation; seizure of goods/documents.
- **s.68 + Rule 138** E-way bill for movement > ₹50,000. **s.129** detention (penalty 200% of tax) and **s.130** confiscation are the enforcement teeth (see next page).
- **s.69** Arrest powers (cognizable/non-bailable only for >₹5 crore fraud offences under s.132).
- **s.70** Summons; **s.71** access to business premises; **s.72** officer assistance.

## Chapter XV — Demands and Recovery (ss. 73–84)

### The three demand regimes

| | s.73 (no fraud) | s.74 (fraud/suppression) | **s.74A (from FY 2024-25)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applies to | Periods up to FY 2023-24 | Periods up to FY 2023-24 | **FY 2024-25 onwards — all cases** |
| SCN time limit | 33 months from annual-return due date | 54 months | **42 months** (both fraud & non-fraud) |
| Order time limit | 3 years | 5 years | **12 months from SCN** (+6 months ext.) |
| Penalty (normal) | 10% of tax (min ₹10,000) | 100% | 10% non-fraud / 100% fraud |
| Pay before SCN | No penalty | 15% penalty | same pattern |
| Pay within 30 days of SCN | No penalty | 25% | same pattern (60 days under 74A) |
| Pay within 30 days of order | — | 50% | 50% (fraud), 60 days |

- **Section 75** — general provisions: personal hearing mandatory where adverse order proposed; interest self-attaches; **75(12)** allows direct recovery of *self-assessed* tax declared in GSTR-1 but not paid in 3B (DRC-01B/1C intercept this on the portal).
- **Section 76** — tax collected but not paid: recoverable regardless of limitation.
- **Section 78/79 — Recovery**: 3 months after order (earlier if justified) → recovery via deduction, detaining goods, **garnishee notices (DRC-13) to debtors/banks**, property attachment.
- **Section 80** — instalments (up to 24) for non-self-assessed dues.
- **Section 83** — provisional attachment of property/bank accounts (1-year life).
- **Section 84** — continuation of recovery on appeal-stage reduction.
- **Section 128A (cross-ref)** — amnesty: interest & penalty waiver for **s.73 demands of FY 2017-18 to 2019-20** where tax paid by the notified date. Pair with s.16(5) for ITC-time-limit demands.

### Notice-handling SOP (fits the IT & GST case tracker)

1. Log every ASMT-10/DRC-01A/DRC-01/ADT-01 with statutory reply date.
2. Check the demand's period → correct regime (73/74/74A) and whether limitation has lapsed.
3. Quantify pre-deposit/early-payment options — 74A's graded penalties reward early payment.
4. Reply on the portal (DRC-06); always seek personal hearing (s.75(4)).
