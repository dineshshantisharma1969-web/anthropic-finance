### GST updates

- **AATO amendment window open 1–31 July 2026** for FY 2025-26 on the GST portal; from 1 July, Aggregate Annual Turnover auto-updates as returns are filed. Officials will review amended AATO data 1–15 August. *Action: verify each ISPL GSTIN's turnover figure before 31 July.*
- **e-Invoice / e-Way Bill API changes from 1 August 2026** — GSTN advisory: **Ship-to GSTIN becomes mandatory** in IRN and e-way bill APIs whenever ship-to information is present. Billing software must be patched before August. *(Also flagged by GST Platform as "new sale reco from Aug 2026" and "billing big change".)*
- **Rate structure (recap):** two main slabs — 5% and 18% — plus 40% demerit rate, in force since 22 Sep 2025 (GST 2.0). Manpower/security/housekeeping services remain **18%**.
- **GST turnover limits** — GST Platform (13 Jul video) flags changes to GST limits with a 31.07.2026 date; verify against the actual notification before acting.

### Income tax updates

- **Income-tax Act, 2025 is in force from 1 April 2026**, but **AY 2026-27 returns (being filed now, for FY 2025-26) are still governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961** — this is the last filing season under the 1961 Act.
- **New form numbering under the 2025 Act** is live for current-year compliance: TDS returns now on Forms 138/140/143/144, challan-cum-statement Form 141, and Form 168 replacing 26AS-style credit statements on the portal (per CA Guru Ji walkthroughs).
- **CBDT Notification 80/2026**: TDS exemption for specified payments (interest, dividend, professional fees, commission, brokerage) received by eligible IFSC units.
- **AIS now shows foreign assets information** — a significant disclosure-matching signal for ITR filers (flagged by CA Guru Ji, 16–17 Jul).

### Case-law highlights (July 2026)

- **Bombay HC**: excess ITC availed beyond GSTR-2A + RCM evasion can justify the extended-period (fraud) demand; the six-month gap between SCN and order under the statute held **directory, not mandatory**.
- **Tripura HC** (*Sri Shekhar Chandra Podder v. UoI*): retrospective cancellation of GST registration upheld; constitutional challenge to s.29 rejected.
- **Gujarat HC** (pending): GST on **corporate guarantees** given by holding companies without consideration — outcome will matter for group-company guarantee structures.
- **Supreme Court** (line of ITC rulings): ITC recognised as a **vested right**; credit cannot be denied merely because the supplier later cancelled registration.
