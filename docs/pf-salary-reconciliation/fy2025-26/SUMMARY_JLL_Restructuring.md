# JLL — Code on Wages 50% Restructuring Annexure

Client: **Jones Lang LaSalle Property Consultants (India) Pvt Ltd** — ISPL's
largest wage-code exposure. Basis: Code on Wages 2019 s.2(y) (in force 21-11-2025),
remuneration per MoLE FAQ 16.03.2026. Structure basis month: March-26 (latest).

## Method
Raise each employee's **Basic+DA to 50% of remuneration** (= CTC − gratuity
provision − employer ESI); reduce other allowances by the same amount so **CTC
and employee gross stay unchanged**. Employer cost rises only on PF (13%, capped
at the ₹15,000 PF wage ceiling) and gratuity accrual (4.81%, no ceiling).

## Headline
- Employees to restructure: **241** (persistent failers present in March-26)
- Total Basic+DA to shift into wages: significant (avg ~₹10-33k/head)
- **Extra employer cost: ₹60,318/month = ₹7.24 lakh/year**
  - of which PF (13%, ceiling-capped): **₹18,359/mo**
  - of which gratuity (4.81%): **₹41,959/mo**
- Extra employee PF deduction (take-home ↓, retiral ↑): ₹16,946/mo

## Key insight — the PF ceiling caps the real cost
Most JLL failers are **high-CTC / low-basic** staff whose Basic+DA already exceeds
the ₹15,000 PF ceiling. Raising their basic further adds **only gratuity (4.81%)**,
not PF — so gratuity is 70% of the cost, not PF. A naive "13%+4.81% on the shortfall"
badly overstates the bill. This is the number to price into the JLL bill rate:
**≈₹7.2L/year**, not the ₹20L+ a gross-up would imply.

## Action
1. Restructure from **21-11-2025** (law date) — gratuity already accrues on the
   deemed-higher basic; delay = arrears exposure.
2. Recover ≈₹60k/month via JLL bill-rate uplift (annexure tab has per-employee cost).
3. Communicate the take-home dip to employees before rollout (CTC unchanged; the
   dip is extra PF saved to their own account).
