**Note on the 690 above-ceiling rows:** ESI's ₹21,000 is an *eligibility threshold*, not a wage cap.
An employee who crosses ₹21,000 mid contribution-period (Apr–Sep / Oct–Mar) stays covered — and
contributes on full wages — until the period ends. So above-ceiling ESI is generally **legitimate
continuation**, not an error; listed per month in the `ABOVE_21000` column for confirmation.

## 5. Scope note

This validates and reconciles the **filed ESIC register** (the deposited position) against the
salary population and the statutory rules. The skill's row-level salary-side ESI *adjustment*
passes (E1–E4: aligning per-site salary ESI, zeroing multi-site secondaries, day-tinkering) act on
the **full salary sheet's** ESI columns, which live in the >10 MB salary workbook that exceeds the
Drive connector cap — those edits must be run locally if required. The filed-position reconciliation,
statutory validation, West-only confirmation, ceiling analysis and coverage-gap identification are
complete here.
